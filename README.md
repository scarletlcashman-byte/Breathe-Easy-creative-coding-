# Breathe-Easy-creative-coding-
Breathe Easy is a calming piece of music I coded in TunePad using Python, made to help ease anxiety. It’s based on Bill Withers’ Lean on Me, because my research showed that recognisable tunes help settle the mind, and its C major key and 4/4 time are easy for the brain to connect with. The track has four instruments: a bass accompaniment, chords and the chorus melody, all on piano, plus a bass guitar that comes in after the chorus has played through once. I worked from the sheet music, turning each note into its number in the code. I used a loop and a list to keep the chords simple, and a 32-beat rest to bring the bass guitar in at the right moment. It’s a slowed-down, stripped-back version of the original, built as a prototype for my Creative Coding subject at UTS.
